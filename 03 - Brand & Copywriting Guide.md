# Document 03: Brand & Copywriting Guide

## DS Home Services

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Priority:** Critical

## Purpose

This document defines the brand voice, messaging, copywriting principles, and communication style for the entire project.

Every word written on the website must follow these guidelines.

The goal is not to sound like a construction company.

The goal is to sound like a premium, modern service that people instantly trust.

## Brand Personality

- Reliable
- Modern
- Friendly
- Professional
- Fast
- Confident
- Simple
- Human
- Helpful

Never arrogant.

Never corporate.

Never salesy.

Never overly emotional.

## Brand Voice

Speak like a helpful expert.

Not like an advertiser.

Not like a salesman.

Not like a legal document.

Use simple language.

Write naturally.

Be confident without exaggeration.

## Core Message

The company solves everyday home problems.

Quickly.

Professionally.

Without unnecessary stress.

The customer should feel:

> “I finally found one company that can handle everything.”

## Main Value

We don't sell repairs.

We sell peace of mind.

We sell convenience.

We sell saved time.

We sell reliability.

## What We Never Sell

- Luxury
- Cheap prices
- Discounts
- Complex technical details
- Construction expertise
- Industrial work

## What We Always Sell

- Fast response
- Easy communication
- Reliable service
- Professional results
- Convenience

## Copywriting Style

- Short sentences
- Simple words
- No complicated explanations
- No unnecessary adjectives
- No corporate language

Every sentence should move the customer closer to contacting the company.

## Headlines

Headlines must immediately communicate value.

### Good Examples

> One Call.  
> Every Small Home Repair.

> Home Repairs.  
> Done Today.

> Need Something Fixed?

> Your Home.  
> Our Job.

> Small Problems.  
> Professional Solutions.

> Everything Fixed.  
> Without the Hassle.

### Bad Examples

- Professional Construction Company
- High Quality Services
- Leading Industry Experts
- Reliable Solutions Since 2010

## Body Text

Use conversational language.

Explain benefits, not features.

### Example: Electrical

Instead of:

> Professional electrical services.

Write:

> Need a socket replaced?  
> We'll take care of it.

### Example: Furniture Assembly

Instead of:

> Expert furniture assembly.

Write:

> Bought new furniture?  
> We'll assemble it for you.

## Call to Action

CTA buttons should feel easy.

### Good

- Get an Estimate
- Book a Visit
- Contact Us
- Let's Fix It
- Need Help?
- Request Service
- Emergency WhatsApp

### Avoid

- Submit
- Send
- Click Here
- Learn More

## Emotional Triggers

- Save time
- Reduce stress
- One company
- One visit
- Trusted professionals
- Fast help
- Transparent process

## Trust Messaging

Instead of saying:

> We are professional.

Show:

- Fast response
- Real work
- Satisfied customers
- Clear communication
- Professional appearance

## Avoid These Words

- Best
- Cheapest
- Guaranteed
- World Class
- Leading
- Perfect
- Revolutionary
- Unmatched
- Unlimited
- Premium Quality
- Number One

## Use These Words More Often

- Easy
- Quick
- Today
- Reliable
- Professional
- Simple
- Trusted
- Local
- Experienced
- Ready

## How We Describe Services

Never list technical specifications.

Describe real situations.

Instead of:

> Light plumbing services.

Write:

> Leaking faucet?  
> Need a new shower installed?  
> We'll handle it.

## How We Describe Speed

Never promise impossible response times.

### Avoid

- 10 minutes
- 15 minutes
- Instant

### Instead Say

- Fast response
- Same-day availability
- As soon as possible

## How We Talk About the Company

### Use

- We
- Our team
- Our technicians

### Avoid

- The company
- The organization
- The enterprise

## How We Talk About the Customer

Always use:

- You
- Your home
- Your apartment
- Your family

Make communication personal.

## Error Messages

Never sound technical.

Instead of:

> Validation Error.

Write:

> Please enter your phone number.

Instead of:

> Submission failed.

Write:

> Something went wrong.  
> Please try again.

## Success Messages

Friendly.

Simple.

Example:

> Thank you.  
> We've received your request.  
> Our team will contact you shortly.

## Microcopy

Buttons.

Forms.

Labels.

Placeholders.

Tooltips.

Everything should sound human.

Example:

> Phone Number

Instead of:

> Telephone Input

## Final Goal

The visitor should feel like they are talking to real people.

Not reading marketing copy.

Every word should increase trust.

Every sentence should reduce hesitation.

Every button should encourage action without pressure.
