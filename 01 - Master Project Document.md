# Document 01: Master Project Document

## DS Home Services

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Priority:** Critical

## Mission

You are not building a website.

You are creating a premium digital product that represents an entirely new standard for local home repair services.

The website must feel like a technology company rather than a traditional handyman business.

Every design decision, animation, interaction, and piece of copy should reinforce one idea:

> “Getting home repairs should be as easy as ordering an Uber.”

## Project Vision

The company provides fast home repair services across Friuli Venezia Giulia, Italy.

Customers should never have to search for five different specialists.

Instead, they contact one company.

That company solves multiple small home problems in one visit.

The website should immediately communicate simplicity, speed, and trust.
